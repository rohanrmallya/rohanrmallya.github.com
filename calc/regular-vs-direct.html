Calculation Methodology

Daily Compounding Engine

Uses daily iteration (not monthly approximation) for precise NAV drag simulation. Daily rates derived from annual rates: rd = (1+CAGR)^(1/365)-1, cd = Drag/365.

SIP Timing & Step-Up

SIP added on 1st of each calendar month. Annual step-up applied every 12 months. Commission drag applied daily on the full AUM (trailing commission model).

Indian Number Formatting

All currency displayed in Indian numbering system (lakhs/crores) with ₹ symbol. Example: ₹1,00,000 = 1 Lakh, ₹1,00,00,000 = 1 Crore.

Assumptions

365-day year, no exit loads, no transaction costs, constant CAGR & drag. Real returns will vary. This illustrates the mathematical impact of expense ratio differentials.
